# Code of conduct

Rebirth of Civilisation is a small open-source game. Everyone is welcome to use it, report bugs, suggest ideas and contribute.

When you take part in this project's issues, pull requests and discussions, please:

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and help newcomers.
- Keep discussion on topic.
- Do not harass, insult or threaten anyone, and do not post discriminatory or sexual content, or anyone's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block participants who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer, [SplashMurray](https://github.com/SplashMurray), through GitHub.
