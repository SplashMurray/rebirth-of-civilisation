Changes made for Rebirth of Civilisation: files were renamed, some sound effects and ambient
loops were shortened and given fades, everything was resampled to 44.1 kHz and re-encoded as
Ogg Vorbis. These modified files are likewise licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.
The exact sources and conversion are in tools/fetch_audio.sh (0 A.D. git commit
61a3b9507d974084e6badb88a0826bd89a6d5b8b, folder binaries/data/mods/public/audio).
