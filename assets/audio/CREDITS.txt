Rebirth of Civilisation - sound and music credits
==================================================

The audio comes from three kinds of open source. Every file keeps the licence of its source.
Changes made for Rebirth of Civilisation: files were renamed, trimmed, given fades,
loudness-matched, resampled to 44.1 kHz and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis; some primate calls were
pitch-shifted (noted below). Exact sources and steps: tools/fetch_audio.sh.
